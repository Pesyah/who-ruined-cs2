# H2-E7 release readiness

**Recommended for another research review: C1/E2.** Exact trade links and
the debit ceiling provide a narrow, auditable mechanism. Of the tested
changes, E2 has the greatest effect on rank and is responsible for all six
winner changes observed in C7. Review those changed cases against independently
labeled matches before proposing E2 for release.

**Conservative comparison arm: C2/U2.** It changed ten positions and no
awards in the nominal run. Its support input is observable and bounded.
Usage without a verified effect can still receive credit. This creates a
behavioral risk even when additional uses have diminishing value. Retain C0
as the release baseline while that risk is evaluated.

**Defer combined C7 and standalone R2/Economy B release.** The exact context
replacement is technically viable in the shared kernel. The nominal effects
are small, and the available corpus does not establish improvement. In
particular, economy comparison coverage and roster participation vary, and
the old context center was calibrated on the frozen inputs. Retain naïve
E3 effects and additive E5 R1/R3 and E6-C outside the candidate matrix. Use
carryover diagnostically, without assuming save intent. No coefficients are
recommended for production.

## Remaining work before a release decision

- Obtain independent labels and broader match populations, with match,
  player and source overlap audited. Inspect C1 award reversals and ensure
  neutralization does not reward systematically dangerous trades.
- Measure U2 incentives and availability across grenade types. Review
  E5/E6 centering and slices with comparable per-side/economic exposure.
- E8 is ready for candidate review and validation design. The evidence is
  insufficient to activate a new H2 policy or recompute historical awards.

## Separate production data issues

- The parser supplies raw `balance`; the normalized pipeline asks for `cash`.
  Keep the alias correction, parser compatibility and any migration separate.
- The tested observer/pro corpus lacks usable round-end/winner evidence in
  the installed native adapter. Investigate raw stream/parser support before
  attempting to expand production applicability.
- Frozen `LOW_EQUIP_COMBAT` uses incomplete opponent pools and last-write
  snapshots. The E7 policy estimates the effect of replacement. A product
  fix needs its own compatibility and policy decision.
