# H2-E7 joint policy contract

`h2-e7-joint-policy@1-research` accepts a normalized match, frozen prepared
profiles with `countedRoundIds`, E4 round-start evidence and E6 equipment
observations. The runner uses the production sidecar adapter, preserving H2
eligibility and its distinct unknown-objective behavior. Roster IDs bind team
identity. Side is round-specific. Duplicate identical normalized events collapse
by ID. Conflicting values under one event ID are rejected. Snapshot conflicts
are withheld locally at the player/round/phase level.

`prepareJointEvidence` materializes E2 trade links, E3 usage facts, E4 timeline,
E5 overlap ledger and E6 comparison facts once. `projectJointPolicy` receives
that data, an arm C0–C11 and an explicit configuration. It clones each edited
profile. Death edits and utility credits enter the physical family inputs,
and context edits enter the same winner selection, saturation and centering
as frozen H2. Then the common projector applies H2 positive mass, softened
negative mass and direct harm. The common award selector yields hypothetical
awards from the unchanged eligible pool. The frozen model/digest on the inner
kernel identifies the underlying arithmetic. The experimental wrapper and the
runner’s config digest identify the candidate separately.

Each result includes rating/availability, raw family inputs, positive and
negative mass, rating terms, six final family attributions, kernel factors,
awards and an anchor ledger. `1 + sum(finalAttribution)` reproduces the score
within `1e-12`. Non-finite outputs, invalid evidence bindings or pool drift
fail the comparison. C0 equals the unmodified projector result. Production
callers keep the same entry point and pass zero research overrides.

The optional NDJSON runner output contains the complete per-candidate ledger,
projection and awards. The compact run report retains its deterministic C7
digest per match plus aggregate evidence and reasons. Evidence arrays are
only compared on the shared frozen exposure of each player.
